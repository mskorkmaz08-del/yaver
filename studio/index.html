Hand controls

  • 🤏Pinch
    grab an object
  • 🖐️Open hand
    let go
  • 🤏↕Push or pull
    toward the camera sends it away, back brings it closer
  • 🤏🤏Both hands pinch
    scale and rotate
  • 🤏⟳Pinch empty space
    orbit (both hands: zoom)
  • ✌️Peace sign, hold
    add a shape
  • 🤏→🗑️Drop in the bin
    delete
  • ✊Fist on an object, hold
    delete

Mouse: drag to move or orbit, scroll to zoom, Shift + scroll to scale. Keys: 1 to 5 add, Delete removes, H help, C camera.
